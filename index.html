Questions

Are the New Era caps genuine?

Yes. Every New Era cap we sell is an authentic New Era product. Crown & Brim is an independent retailer and is not owned by or affiliated with New Era Cap.

What is the difference between the New Era styles?

59FIFTY is a fitted cap with a flat brim and a structured crown, sold by exact head size. 9FIFTY has the same shape with a snapback closure. 9FORTY has a curved brim and an adjustable strap.

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2pm ship the same working day and usually arrive in 2 to 4 working days.

Can I return something that does not fit?

Yes. Send it back unused with the tags or stickers on within 30 days and we will exchange or refund it.
