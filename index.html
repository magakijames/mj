Questions

Are these genuine New Era caps?

Yes. Every cap we sell is an authentic New Era product. Crown & Brim is an independent retailer and is not owned by or affiliated with New Era Cap.

What is the difference between the styles?

59FIFTY is a fitted cap with a flat brim and a structured crown, sold by exact head size. 9FIFTY has the same shape with a snapback closure. 9FORTY has a curved brim and an adjustable strap. 39THIRTY is a curved-brim cap in stretch fabric, sold in three size bands.

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2pm ship the same working day and usually arrive in 2 to 4 working days.

Can I return a cap that does not fit?

Yes. Send it back unworn with the stickers on within 30 days and we will exchange or refund it.
